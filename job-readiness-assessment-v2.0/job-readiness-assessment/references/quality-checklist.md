# Quality checklist

Run before sharing any report. Each item: Pass / Fail / Not applicable. Any failed **[Critical]** item must be fixed first.

## Integrity
1. **[Critical]** No invented experience, tools, qualifications, metrics or levels.
2. **[Critical]** No invented company facts, internal priorities or interview stages.
3. **[Critical]** Facts, evidence and inferences are visibly tagged (Fact / Analysis / Inference).
4. **[Critical]** Missing evidence shown as "not assessed" or "not evidenced", never as lack of skill.
5. **[Critical]** CV and interview advice never suggests claiming an unearned skill.
6. **[Critical]** No use or inference of age or other protected characteristics; career stage comes from experience only.
7. **[Critical]** Only this person's profile is used.
8. No overall score or percentage.

## Job and employer
9. Title, employer, location, source and date checked are stated; missing information is flagged.
10. Each requirement is required, preferred or contextual; implicit ones cite their rule and are never required.
11. Licences and regulated qualifications are treated as required and verified only from the issuer, a register or a flagged document.
12. **[Critical]** Every current company fact has a source next to it, with a date where relevant.
13. Role implications are labelled Analysis; inaccessible pages are disclosed.

## Matching and plan
14. Each assessed skill cites specific evidence, one evidence label and one verification level.
15. Targets follow the career-stage ceilings, or the reason for a higher target is stated.
16. Each competency shows its status rule and "k of n".
17. Gaps are ranked by the five factors; confirmed gaps are separated from missing evidence.
18. Each plan task has hours, a deliverable and a completion criterion, and fits the person's stated time.
19. Durable human skills are rated only from behavioural examples; wellbeing is not assessed.

## Report
20. **[Critical]** Delivered as the HTML report from the template, or the fallback is used with a reason.
21. **[Critical]** Visuals match the tables (only the data block was edited).
22. Rendered and checked at desktop and phone width.
23. Plain language, in the language requested; states it is AI-assisted guidance.
24. Every source has a URL or reference and a retrieval date.

**Ready to share:** all critical items pass and no more than two others fail. Otherwise revise.
